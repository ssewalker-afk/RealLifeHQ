Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 18, 2026

Introduction

RealLife HQ ("we," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your personal information when you use our iOS application.

Your privacy is paramount to us. All data you create within RealLife HQ is stored exclusively on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any of your personal data with third parties or our servers. When optional features like Apple Calendar Sync or notifications are enabled, they interact only with Apple's on-device frameworks — your data never touches our servers.

Information Stored on Your Device

RealLife HQ stores the following types of data locally on your device using iOS's secure local storage. None of this data is transmitted to us or any third party.

Calendar Events

  • Event titles, dates, times, and notes
  • Completion status and reminder settings
  • Recurring event schedules

Habit Tracking Data

  • Habit names, icons, and colors
  • Completion dates and streak history
  • Frequency, schedule, and reminder settings
  • Free users: up to 3 habits stored locally

Journal Entries

  • Entry content and dates
  • Mood tracking selections
  • Tags for categorization
  • Free users: up to 3 entries stored locally
  • Premium users: When you use the PDF Export feature, you choose where to send the file (print, email, save to Files, etc.). RealLife HQ does not receive or transmit the exported PDF.

Cleaning Tracker Data

  • Cleaning schedule configuration (rooms, days, frequency)
  • Task completion history and streaks
  • Custom task names and assigned days
  • Setup wizard preferences

Life Reminders & Notifications

  • Reminder wizard responses (life category selections: pets, vehicles, health, property, etc.)
  • Generated reminder titles and dates — stored locally and scheduled as iOS local notifications
  • Notification preferences and schedules
  • All notification data remains on your device and is never transmitted

Budget & Financial Information

  • Budget setup and income information
  • Expense transactions and categories
  • Recurring expense schedules
  • Category limits and spending data

Meal Planning & Recipes

  • Recipe names, ingredients, and instructions
  • Cooking times and servings
  • Weekly meal plans and shopping lists
  • Saved and favorite recipe selections

Vault Items (Secure Storage)

  • Item titles and categories
  • Usernames and website URLs
  • Passwords and secure notes — stored in iOS Keychain (hardware-encrypted, never leaves your device)
  • Attached photos (stored as encrypted local data)

App Settings & Preferences

  • Theme and appearance selections
  • Notification and reminder preferences
  • Biometric authentication preferences
  • Subscription status (Free / Monthly / Lifetime) — managed by Apple via StoreKit

Device Permissions We May Request

Certain features require iOS permissions. All permissions are optional — you can use the core app without granting any of them. We never request permissions beyond what is needed for the feature you choose to use.

📅

Calendar Access OPTIONAL PREMIUM
Required only if you enable Apple Calendar Sync in Settings. RealLife HQ uses Apple's EventKit framework to read and write events directly between the app and your on-device Apple Calendar. No calendar data is transmitted to our servers. You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings.

🔔

Notifications OPTIONAL PREMIUM
Required if you enable habit reminders, cleaning reminders, or Life Reminders. All notifications are scheduled locally on your device using Apple's UserNotifications framework. No notification content is transmitted to our servers. You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings.

🔒

Face ID / Touch ID OPTIONAL
Required only if you enable biometric lock for the Vault feature. Authentication is handled entirely by iOS's LocalAuthentication framework. Your biometric data never leaves your device and is never accessible to RealLife HQ.

🖼️

Photo Library OPTIONAL
Required only if you attach photos to Vault items. Selected photos are stored locally within the app's sandboxed storage. We do not access your full photo library — only photos you explicitly choose to attach.

Data NOT Collected

✗

No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, Apple IDs, or any other personal identifiers.

✗

No Location Data: We do not request or collect your location at any time.

✗

No Analytics or Crash Reporting: We do not use third-party analytics services (such as Firebase, Mixpanel, or Amplitude). No usage events, screen views, or crash logs are transmitted.

✗

No Advertising Data: We do not collect any data for advertising or marketing purposes and do not use advertising SDKs.

✗

No Cloud Sync: Your data is not synced to our servers. There is no RealLife HQ account or cloud account of any kind.

✗

No External AI Processing: RealLife HQ does not send any data to external AI providers (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Google). All AI-assisted features, if present, use Apple's on-device frameworks only.

In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

RealLife HQ offers optional Premium subscriptions and a Lifetime Access purchase through Apple's App Store. All purchase transactions are processed entirely by Apple via StoreKit. We do not collect or store your payment information.

  • Apple processes and handles all payment data under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • RealLife HQ only receives a confirmation of your purchase status (active / inactive) from StoreKit — never your payment details.
  • Your subscription status is stored locally on your device by iOS.
  • To manage, change, or cancel a subscription, go to iOS Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions.

Journal PDF Export

Premium users can export journal entries as a PDF. When you tap the Export button, a PDF is generated entirely on your device. You then choose where to send it using the iOS Share Sheet (print, email, save to Files, Messages, etc.).

RealLife HQ does not receive, store, or transmit the exported PDF. Any sharing that occurs is solely between you and the destination you choose — we have no involvement in or visibility into that transfer.

Apple Calendar Sync

Premium users may enable Apple Calendar Sync. When enabled, RealLife HQ uses Apple's EventKit framework to read from and write events to your on-device Apple Calendar. This sync is entirely local — calendar data is exchanged directly between RealLife HQ and Apple Calendar on your device. No calendar events or data are transmitted to our servers.

You can disable Apple Calendar Sync at any time in Settings > Integrations, and you can revoke calendar access in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Calendars.

How We Use Your Information

All data you create within RealLife HQ is:

1.

Stored Locally: All information is stored exclusively on your device using iOS's secure local storage mechanisms (UserDefaults and iOS Keychain for sensitive items).

2.

User-Controlled: You have complete control over your data and can view, edit, or delete it at any time within the app.

3.

Never Transmitted to Us: Your data is never sent to RealLife HQ servers, cloud services, or third parties.

4.

Not Shared or Sold: We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your information with anyone, ever.

Data Security

RealLife HQ implements multiple layers of security to protect your information:

🔒

iOS Keychain Protection

All passwords and secure notes from the Vault are stored in iOS Keychain — Apple's most secure storage system. This data is encrypted at the hardware level and protected by your device's security features.

🔒

Local Storage Security

App data is stored in iOS's secure sandboxed local storage. All data remains on your device and cannot be accessed by other apps.

🔒

Biometric Protection for Vault

The Vault feature supports optional Face ID / Touch ID protection via iOS's LocalAuthentication framework. Your biometric data is processed entirely by the Secure Enclave and never exposed to the app.

🔒

Device-Level Encryption

All data stored on your device benefits from iOS's built-in hardware encryption when your device is locked, providing an additional layer of protection.

🔒

No Network Transmission

Because your data never leaves your device to our servers, it cannot be intercepted in transit or accessed through a server breach.

✓

Third-Party Services

RealLife HQ does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or data platforms. The app uses only standard Apple iOS frameworks:

  • StoreKit 2 — Handles in-app purchases and subscription validation, managed entirely by Apple.
  • EventKit — Used for Apple Calendar Sync (Premium). All data exchange is on-device only.
  • UserNotifications — Schedules local notifications for reminders (Premium). All data stays on device.
  • iOS Keychain — Securely stores Vault passwords and secure notes on-device.
  • LocalAuthentication — Handles Face ID / Touch ID for Vault protection, processed by iOS Secure Enclave.
  • PhotosUI — Provides photo picker access for Vault attachments. Only explicitly selected photos are used.

No third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, advertising frameworks, or cloud services are included in RealLife HQ.

iCloud Backup

If you have iCloud Backup enabled on your device, your RealLife HQ app data (excluding Vault Keychain items) will be included in your device's automatic iCloud backup. This backup is managed entirely by Apple and is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. RealLife HQ does not control or initiate iCloud backups — this is an iOS system feature you can manage in iOS Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud > iCloud Backup.

Children's Privacy

Parents can allow children to use the app with confidence that no data is being collected or transmitted externally.

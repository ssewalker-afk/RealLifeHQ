📖 Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How do I sync with Apple Calendar?
Go to Settings → Integrations → Apple Calendar Sync and enable the toggle. You'll be asked to grant calendar access permissions. Once enabled, your RealLifeHQ events will sync bidirectionally with Apple Calendar.
❓ How does the Cleaning Tracker work?
The Cleaning Tracker helps you maintain a clean home with scheduled tasks. Set up your cleaning schedule in the wizard, and the app will assign tasks daily. Complete tasks to build streaks and track your progress over time.
❓ What is the Life Reminder Wizard?
The Life Reminder Wizard asks you questions about your life (pets, vehicles, health, etc.) and automatically creates important recurring reminders for things like vet visits, oil changes, tax deadlines, and more.
❓ How do I use the Recipe & Meal Planning features?
Add recipes manually or import them. Create meal plans by scheduling recipes for specific dates and meal types (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack). Shopping list items are automatically generated from your meal plans and can be checked off as you shop.
❓ How do I back up my data?
Your data is stored locally on your device for maximum privacy and security. All data is automatically included in your device's iCloud backup if enabled. There is no cloud sync to protect your privacy.
❓ How do I set up recurring events?
When creating or editing an event, toggle 'Recurring Event' and choose your preferred frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly). You can also use the Life Reminder Wizard to set up common recurring events automatically.
❓ What's the difference between Events and Habits?
Events are one-time or recurring appointments with specific dates and times. Habits are daily activities you want to track and build streaks for. Think of Events as your schedule and Habits as your goals.
❓ How secure is the Vault?
Vault passwords and secure notes are stored in iOS Keychain, Apple's most secure storage system, with hardware-level encryption. You can enable Face ID/Touch ID for additional protection. All data stays on your device and is never transmitted.
❓ Can I use RealLifeHQ offline?
Yes! RealLifeHQ is designed to work completely offline. All your data is stored locally on your device for maximum privacy and reliability. No internet connection is required for any feature.
❓ How do Budget Categories work?
Set up your monthly budget with income and category limits. Track expenses by category throughout the month. The Budget view shows remaining amounts, spending trends, and alerts you when approaching limits.
❓ Can I set recurring expenses?
Yes! When adding an expense, toggle 'Recurring' and choose the frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly). Recurring expenses are automatically added to your budget calculations.
❓ How do I change my subscription?
You can manage your subscription through the App Store. Go to iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → RealLifeHQ to change plans, cancel, or restore purchases.
❓ How do I delete my data?
Go to Settings → Data → Clear All Data. This will permanently delete all your events, habits, journal entries, recipes, meal plans, budget data, and vault items. This action cannot be undone.
❓ Why am I not receiving notifications?
Check that notifications are enabled in both iOS Settings → Notifications → RealLifeHQ and within the app at Settings → Notifications. Also ensure Do Not Disturb/Focus modes are not blocking notifications.
❓ What data does RealLifeHQ collect?
None! All your data stays on your device. We don't collect, transmit, or share any personal information. See our Privacy Policy for complete details on our privacy-first approach.
❓ Can I customize the app theme?
Yes! Go to Settings → Theme to choose from multiple color themes. Pick colors that match your style and make the app feel personalized to you.

🚀 Getting Started

New to RealLifeHQ?

Here's a quick guide to get you started:

  1. Set Your Theme: Go to Settings → Theme and pick colors you love
  2. Add Your First Event: Tap Calendar → + to create an event with a reminder
  3. Use the Life Reminder Wizard: In Calendar, tap "Life Reminders" to automatically set up important recurring reminders
  4. Create a Habit: Go to Habits → + and set up a daily habit you want to track
  5. Set Up Cleaning Schedule: Use the Cleaning Tracker wizard to create your personalized cleaning routine
  6. Write a Journal Entry: Visit the Journal tab and respond to today's prompt
  7. Set Up Your Budget: In Budget, set your monthly budget and category limits, then start tracking expenses

🔧 Troubleshooting

Calendar Sync Issues

If calendar sync isn't working:

  • Check that you've granted calendar permissions in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars
  • Disable and re-enable the sync in RealLifeHQ Settings → Integrations
  • Make sure you're signed into iCloud on your device
  • Verify that the calendar you selected in settings still exists
  • Try creating a test event to see if it syncs

Notification Problems

If you're not receiving notifications:

  • Enable notifications in iOS Settings → Notifications → RealLifeHQ
  • Check that Focus modes (Do Not Disturb, Sleep, Work) are not blocking notifications
  • Verify notification settings in RealLifeHQ Settings → Notifications
  • Make sure you've set reminders on your events and habits
  • Check that notification time preferences are set correctly
  • Try restarting your device

Cleaning Tracker Issues

If cleaning tasks aren't appearing:

  • Make sure you've completed the Cleaning Setup Wizard
  • Check that tasks are enabled in your cleaning schedule
  • Verify that today is not set as a rest day in your schedule
  • Try refreshing the view by navigating away and back

Budget Tracking Issues

If budget calculations seem incorrect:

  • Verify your monthly income is set correctly in Budget Setup
  • Check that all expenses are categorized properly
  • Review recurring expenses - they're calculated for the full month
  • Make sure you're viewing the correct month
  • Check category limits are set as intended

Vault Access Problems

If you can't access the Vault:

  • Make sure Face ID/Touch ID is enabled on your device
  • Check that RealLifeHQ has permission to use biometrics in iOS Settings
  • Try disabling and re-enabling biometric protection in Settings
  • If biometrics fail repeatedly, use your device passcode

App Performance

If the app is running slowly:

  • Close and restart the app by swiping up from the app switcher
  • Update to the latest version from the App Store
  • Restart your device
  • Check available storage space on your device
  • If issues persist after updating, contact support

Data & Backup

Important information about your data:

  • All data is stored locally on your device for privacy
  • Enable iCloud Backup in iOS Settings to protect your data
  • Deleting the app will remove all local data permanently
  • There is no cloud sync - data only exists on your device
  • Clear All Data in Settings is permanent and cannot be undone
